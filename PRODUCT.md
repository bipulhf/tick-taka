# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person (the owner) juggling two remote jobs, teaching, research and home. Uses the app on an Android phone many times a day in short bursts: one-handed on a rickshaw or at a shop counter in daylight, between work blocks at a desk, and in bed at night for the shutdown. Never shared, never onboarded.

## Product Purpose

Tick & Taka runs the day and the money from one screen. Open it and see what to do next and how much is safe to spend; log a task, expense, income or time entry in under five seconds. Success is still opening it every day after three months, because logging feels quick and rewarding instead of like a chore.

## Brand Personality

Cheerful pocket companion: warm, calm, encouraging. Tiki (a coin with a clock face) celebrates small wins and shrugs at missed days; it never scolds. Copy is short and friendly ("Safe to spend today: ৳640").

## Anti-references

- Feature-overloaded planners where every screen shows every option (TickTick's main complaint).
- Small, dense, form-like screens that feel like a web admin panel squeezed onto a phone.
- Rows of colourful chips and many equal-weight cards competing for attention.
- Guilt UI: red warnings, overdue alarms, broken-streak shame.
- Bank-app navy-and-gold seriousness.

## Design Principles

1. **Today first, one glance.** The home screen answers "what now?" and "can I afford it?" before anything else; detail is one tap away, never on top.
2. **Capture beats organise.** Adding something is one field and one button; choices appear only when the parser needs help.
3. **Big, calm, thumb-sized.** Large type, generous space, primary actions in the lower half, every target at least 48 dp.
4. **Colour means something.** One accent for the main action; blue for time, green and coral for money in and out, purple for habits. Everything else stays neutral.
5. **Reward, never guilt.** Celebrate progress briefly; treat misses gently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes; dark text on the mango accent. Touch targets ≥ 48 dp. Respects reduce-motion. Colour is never the only signal (amounts carry a sign, states carry labels or icons). Tabular digits for amounts and timers.
