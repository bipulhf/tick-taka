UX-032 · The Undo snackbar has a fixed 8-second limit

Medium Accessibility / timing (WCAG 2.2.1) · New
Where
components/ui/snackbar.tsx:9-25 (timers), :33 (position), :35-55
What's wrong
The snackbar closes after 5 s, or 8 s with an action, whatever the user's setting. Android has an accessibility "Time to take action" setting, which RN exposes as AccessibilityInfo.getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(). There are 0 uses of it in the repo. The live region reads only the message ("Deleted “Rent”"), so a TalkBack user doesn't hear that an Undo exists and must swipe to it before it goes. The bar also sits at insets.bottom + 92, which overlaps the running-timer bar (inset + 84 to inset + 140) and hides its Stop button while shown Unverified.
Evidence
const VISIBLE_MS = 5000;
/** An Undo needs time to notice and reach for. */
const WITH_ACTION_MS = 8000;
...
snack.onAction ? WITH_ACTION_MS : VISIBLE_MS,
Why it matters
Undo replaces "Are you sure?" for every delete, completion, snooze, bulk move and quick-add. For a screen-reader user, an Undo that times out means there is no safeguard.
Recommendation
await AccessibilityInfo.getRecommendedTimeoutMillis(WITH_ACTION_MS), and while a screen reader is on, keep the snackbar until dismissed or until the next one replaces it. Put the action in the spoken text: "Deleted “Rent”. Undo available." When a timer is running, lift the snackbar above RUNNING_TIMER_SPACE.

9. Three bigger redesign suggestions

R1. A widget built from the app's tokens (UX-031, UX-041, UX-033)

Generate the widget's colours from themedPalette() at sync time and store them in the widget cache with the numerals choice. That way the widget can never drift from the tested tokens and follows the accent theme. Lay it out at two heights. Compact: safe-to-spend (hero, mint-text) and "Next: …". Tall: the same, plus a row of 48 dp actions. Make quick-log a two-step tap ("Cha ৳20" turns into "Log ৳20?" for 3 s) or follow it with an Undo status.

R2. One "reversible write" pattern for money (UX-033, UX-032, UX-034)

The app already has the parts: ids made on the phone, an outbox that sends in order, and bulk-move replies with a before state. Standardise them. Every money write from a one-tap control (bill Paid/Received, widget quick-log, quick-add, AI draft Save) returns enough to reverse it. The client always shows the same snackbar with Undo, held open for screen-reader users. Keep one irreversible flow, account deletion, and give it real friction: its own page, export inline, and typed confirmation.

R3. Finish the design system's last mile (UX-007, UX-013, UX-036, UX-040)

Turn the DESIGN.md rules into code that is hard to misuse. ListRow and ShortcutRow default to muted icons and take a domain ("time" | "money-in" | "money-out" | "habit") instead of a raw colour. A ChoiceField wraps PickerField for single choice, with radiogroup semantics, and replaces the remaining chip rows. A Biome rule or unit test flags Pressable without min-h-12/h-12/hitSlop.

10. Strengths (keep these)

  • Tested, documented colour system. Two tiers per semantic colour, line-strong for boundaries, mango-inverse for the snackbar. DESIGN.md, palette.ts and global.css agree exactly, and test/contrast.test.ts checks text, tints, fills, boundaries and both accent themes. Every in-app text pair now passes AA (Appendix A).
  • Data safety around sessions. Sign-out asks first, counts unsynced changes and offers "Try syncing". A 401 keeps the outbox, and the login screen names the account and the waiting changes (app/login.tsx:100-106). A different account wipes the phone first.
  • Undo that names what happened: "Saved: Expense ৳250 · Food · Cash", "Moved “Call bank”, “Read” and 2 more to today", "Reset Drink water", "Done: “…”". Habit over-tap protection and "Take one back".
  • AI money changes are proposals. Draft cards show a signed amount, account, category and date, and nothing is written until Save. Deletions need an explicit Delete, and every applied change has an Undo row.
  • Screen-reader work done properly. The safe-to-spend label carries the numbers. Swipe actions are claimed by the row's focusable element (useSwipeRowA11y). Planner cards offer "Schedule for…" actions. TextField labels are linked, errors use live regions, and the focus timer speaks at milestones only. Most of these have a pure, tested helper (a11y-actions.test.ts, safe-to-spend-label.test.ts, focus-announcement.test.ts).
  • Calm, central error copy in lib/error-copy.ts, with codes mapped to plain sentences and the dev host shown only in __DEV__.
  • Localisation. Lakh grouping by default, Bangla digits and "টাকা" accepted in every parser, and an optional Bangla display for amounts.
  • Font scaling plan. Scaling stays on. Only the hero, tab labels, calendar numbers, keypad, focus clock and emoji circles are capped at 1.3×, and containers use min-h.
  • Reduce motion is respected in count-ups, confetti, Tiki, skeletons, the plant and the thinking dots.
